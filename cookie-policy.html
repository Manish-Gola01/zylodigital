Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 1 October 2026

At Zylo Digital, we use cookies and similar technologies to help our website function properly, understand how visitors use our website, improve user experience, and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we may use them, and the choices available to you.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They allow websites to remember information about your visit, recognise returning visitors, maintain certain website functions, and understand how the website is being used.

We may also use similar technologies such as pixels, tags, scripts, and web beacons.

2. Why We Use Cookies

Zylo Digital may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies

These cookies help our website operate properly. They may be used for:

  • Website functionality
  • Security
  • Form functionality
  • Session management
  • Basic website operations

These cookies may be necessary for certain parts of the website to function.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. We may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Traffic sources
  • Device and browser information
  • Website interactions
  • General website usage patterns

This information helps us improve our website and services.

Advertising & Marketing Cookies

We may use advertising technologies to measure campaigns and understand how visitors interact with advertisements. Depending on the tools implemented on our website, these may include:

  • Google Ads
  • Meta Pixel
  • Meta Ads
  • Conversion tracking
  • Remarketing technologies

These technologies may help us understand campaign performance and deliver more relevant advertising.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services that we use on our website.

These services may include analytics, advertising, video, social media, hosting, or other technology providers.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples may include:

  • Google
  • Meta
  • YouTube
  • Other analytics or advertising platforms

The actual third-party services used on the website may change over time.

4. How Long Cookies Stay on Your Device

Cookies may be either:

  • Session Cookies: These are generally deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

The duration depends on the type and purpose of the cookie.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Allow cookies from selected websites
  • Block third-party cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality or user experience.

6. Cookie Consent

Where applicable, Zylo Digital may display a cookie consent banner allowing visitors to manage certain categories of cookies.

Where consent is required by applicable law, relevant non-essential cookies may only be activated after the appropriate consent is provided.

7. Advertising Choices

If you interact with advertising or tracking technologies provided by third parties, those providers may offer their own tools for managing advertising preferences.

Your browser and device may also provide controls for limiting certain forms of tracking.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technology, services, or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised “Last Updated” date.
