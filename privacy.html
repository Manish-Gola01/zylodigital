Privacy & Legal Framework

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 October 2026

At Zylo Digital, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how Zylo Digital (“Zylo Digital”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website, contact us, request our services, or interact with our digital platforms.

By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Full name
  • Business or company name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Website or social media profile
  • Project requirements
  • Budget or service preferences
  • Messages and other information submitted through our contact forms

We may also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website
  • Approximate location information
  • Date and time of website visits
  • Website usage and interaction data

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and consultation requests
  • Understand your project requirements
  • Prepare proposals, quotations, and project plans
  • Provide our services
  • Communicate with you regarding your project
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Analyse website traffic and performance
  • Send relevant business communications where permitted
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, or security threats
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell your personal information as a product to third parties.

3. Contact Forms

When you submit information through a form on our website, the information may be used by Zylo Digital to contact you regarding your enquiry, requested service, or potential project.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through our general contact forms.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage, measure advertising performance, and provide a better user experience.

Depending on the tools implemented on our website, these technologies may include analytics, advertising, conversion tracking, or other measurement tools. You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality. For detailed information on how we use cookies and manage your preferences, please refer to our dedicated Cookie Policy.

5. Analytics and Advertising

We may use third-party services such as website analytics and advertising platforms to understand website traffic, measure campaigns, and improve our marketing activities. These services may collect information about your interaction with our website in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

If implemented on our website, these services may include platforms such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Google Ads
  • Meta Pixel
  • Meta Ads
  • Other analytics or advertising technologies

The actual third-party services used may change over time.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our business or provide requested services. These may include:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Website and software service providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising platforms
  • Communication and email providers
  • Payment or invoicing providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required by law

We expect service providers handling information on our behalf to use it only for legitimate business purposes and to maintain appropriate security measures.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal obligations. The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, payment services, or other external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those websites before providing them with personal information.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not intentionally directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request information about how your data is being processed
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications

To exercise an applicable privacy right, contact us using the details below.

12. Marketing Communications

If you provide your contact information to Zylo Digital, we may contact you regarding your enquiry, project, services, or relevant business communications. Where required, we will provide an appropriate option to opt out of promotional communications. You can also contact us directly if you no longer wish to receive marketing communications from us.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices. When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
