Engineered Methodology

Our 6-Step Growth Framework

We don't believe in random guessing or generic templates. Every high-converting website, visual asset, and advertising campaign follows an engineered 6-phase journey.

PHASE 01

Discover

Days 1 – 2 • Foundations
🔍

Understanding Brand DNA, Goals & Market Positioning

We start by conducting an in-depth exploration into your business model, customer psychology, competitors, and commercial objectives. We don’t write a single line of code or generate visuals until we know exactly what will make your brand win.

Key Deliverables in Phase 01:
  • Comprehensive Brand Questionnaire
  • Competitor Landscape & Gap Audit
  • Customer Persona & Buying Triggers
  • Technical Requirements & Scope Document
PHASE 02

Strategize

Days 3 – 4 • Architecture
📐

Creative Direction, UX Wireframes & Ad Funnel Planning

With the discovery blueprint in hand, we engineer the creative direction, user journey, visual moodboards, and advertising acquisition architecture. Every touchpoint is planned for trust, speed, and conversion.

Key Deliverables in Phase 02:
  • Creative Direction & Visual Moodboards
  • Information Architecture & Wireframes
  • Conversion Funnel Mapping
  • Paid Acquisition Strategy (Google & Meta)
PHASE 03

Create

Days 5 – 10 • Production
⚡

Design, Development, AI Generation, CGI & Video Production

The heaviest production phase where our team crafts your responsive digital storefront, generates high-end AI product/model photoshoots, animates 3D CGI visuals, and edits dynamic social media reels.

Key Deliverables in Phase 03:
  • Responsive Web Frontend & CMS Integration
  • Up to 200 AI Product/Model Photos
  • CGI 3D Animations & Logo Motion
  • Social Media Reels & Dynamic Ad Creatives
PHASE 04

Launch

Days 11 – 14 • Go-Live
🚀

Deployment, QA, Pixel Tracking & Public Reveal

We rigorously test cross-browser compatibility, mobile performance, SSL security, and Core Web Vitals speed. Once pixel tracking (Meta CAPI & Google Tags) is verified, your brand officially goes live.

Key Deliverables in Phase 04:
  • Production Web Deployment & Domain Setup
  • 100% Core Web Vitals Validation (<300ms)
  • Meta Pixel, CAPI & Google Tag Manager Sync
  • Ad Campaigns Published & Live Tracking
PHASE 05

Optimize

Post-Launch • Performance
📊

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), A/B Testing & Analytics

Launch day is only the start. We examine live user behavioral heatmaps, click rates, drop-off points, and ad engagement. We continuously run A/B copy and creative tests to lower customer acquisition costs.

Key Deliverables in Phase 05:
  • Heatmap & Session Recording Insights
  • Ad Creative & Headline A/B Testing
  • Cart & Lead Form Conversion Improvements
  • Weekly Real-Time Analytics Reporting
PHASE 06

Grow

Scale • Long-Term Value
📈

Scaling Paid Ads, AI Calling Agents & Content Expansion

Once winning ad sets and sales funnels are identified, we increase ad spend profitably. We layer in automated AI calling agents, WhatsApp bots, and recurring content engines to expand brand reach globally.

Key Deliverables in Phase 06:
  • Profitable Meta & Google Ad Scaling (ROAS Focus)
  • AI Calling & 24/7 Appointment Booking Automations
  • Monthly Creative Refresh & New Reels
  • Strategic Quarterly Growth Consultations
