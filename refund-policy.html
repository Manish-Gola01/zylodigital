Billing & Assurance

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 1 October 2026

At Zylo Digital, our philosophy is anchored in fairness, accountability, and partnership. Because we provide customized creative design, custom code engineering, AI visual production, and media creation, services cannot be "returned" like physical merchandise.

Instead, we have structured our billing to eliminate client risk through our Milestone Protection Model.

1. The ₹15,000 Milestone Advance & Balance Protection

How You Are Protected:

You are never asked to pay 100% upfront. Work commences with an initial commitment advance of ₹15,000, which pays for in-depth discovery, brand audit, initial UI/UX wireframes, and production team reservation.

Balance Fee Protection: The remaining contract balance is due only after you have reviewed staging links, received visual assets, and approved the project for final handover.

2. Cancellation Windows

  • Within 48 Hours of Advance Payment: If you notify us in writing within 48 hours of payment before discovery research or wireframes have commenced, 100% of your advance payment will be refunded minus any applicable bank transaction processing fees.
  • During Phase 01 & Phase 02 (Discover & Strategize): If you choose to terminate after discovery research or initial wireframes have been delivered, the ₹15,000 advance is retained to compensate for creative labor and intellectual property already transferred, but you will owe ₹0 for the remaining balance.
  • During Production (Phase 03): If you request project cancellation mid-production, work stops immediately. You will retain all draft assets produced to that date, and no balance fees beyond completed deliverables will be charged.

3. Revisions & Quality Guarantee

If any deliverable does not match your creative expectations, we resolve it through dedicated revision rounds rather than abandonment:

  • Every project includes structured feedback rounds where our designers and engineers refine colors, layouts, typography, and AI composition.
  • We iterate until the delivered product aligns with the agreed scope.

4. Non-Refundable Third-Party Disbursements

Certain third-party costs incurred directly on your behalf cannot be refunded under any circumstances once purchased:

  • Domain name registrations and renewals (managed directly through domain registries).
  • Cloud server, VPS, and web hosting fees paid to infrastructure providers.
  • Paid advertising budget consumed on Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) or Google Ads.
  • Third-party premium software plugins, Shopify apps, or font licenses purchased for your specific site.

5. Ongoing Monthly Management (Ads & Automation)

For monthly recurring services such as Google & Meta Ads Management or AI Automation Maintenance:

  • Services operate on a 30-day billing cycle.
  • You may cancel at any time with 7 days written notice prior to the start of the next 30-day billing cycle.
  • No pro-rated refunds are issued for partially elapsed monthly retainers once ad monitoring and management work has commenced for that cycle.

6. How to Request Cancellation

To submit a cancellation or inquiry regarding a refund, please send an official written request from the email address associated with your project:

Billing & Compliance Department:
Email: supportzylo.digital@gmail.com
Subject Line: Project Cancellation Request • [Your Brand Name]
Response Time: Within 2 business days. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment source within 5–7 business days.
